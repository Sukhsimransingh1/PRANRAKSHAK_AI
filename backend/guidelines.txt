PRANRAKSHAK CLINICAL GUIDELINES — SEPSIS DECISION SUPPORT

SEPSIS-3 DEFINITION (2016 International Consensus):
- Sepsis: life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by dysregulated host response to infection.
- Defined as SOFA score increase of 2 or more points + suspected infection.
- Septic shock: sepsis + vasopressor requirement + lactate > 2 mmol/L despite fluid resuscitation.

QUICK SOFA (qSOFA) — BEDSIDE SCREENING:
Score 1 point each for:
- Altered mentation (GCS < 15)
- Respiratory rate >= 22 breaths/min
- Systolic blood pressure <= 100 mmHg
qSOFA >= 2 warrants further assessment for organ dysfunction.

KEY CLINICAL PARAMETERS AND CONCERN THRESHOLDS:
Heart Rate (HR):        Normal 60-100 bpm.   Concern: > 90 bpm (tachycardia)
Systolic BP (SBP):      Normal 90-120 mmHg.  Concern: < 90 mmHg (hypotension)
Mean Arterial Pressure: Normal 70-100 mmHg.  Concern: < 65 mmHg (shock)
Respiratory Rate:       Normal 12-20/min.    Concern: > 22/min
Temperature:            Normal 36.5-37.5°C.  Concern: > 38.3°C or < 36°C
Oxygen Saturation:      Normal > 95%.        Concern: < 94%
WBC Count:              Normal 4-11 x10^9/L. Concern: > 12 or < 4
Lactate:                Normal < 2 mmol/L.   Concern: > 2 mmol/L (tissue hypoperfusion)
Creatinine:             Normal 0.6-1.2 mg/dL.Concern: > 2.0 mg/dL (AKI)
Glucose:                Normal 70-140 mg/dL. Concern: > 180 mg/dL (stress hyperglycemia)

SURVIVING SEPSIS CAMPAIGN — HOUR-1 BUNDLE:
1. Measure lactate. Re-measure if initial lactate > 2 mmol/L.
2. Obtain blood cultures before administering antibiotics.
3. Administer broad-spectrum antibiotics.
4. Begin rapid administration of 30 mL/kg crystalloid for hypotension or lactate >= 4 mmol/L.
5. Apply vasopressors if patient is hypotensive during or after fluid resuscitation to maintain MAP >= 65 mmHg.

RISK LEVEL INTERPRETATION:
HIGH (probability > 0.75):   Immediate clinical review required. Consider Hour-1 Bundle initiation.
MEDIUM (0.40–0.75):          Close monitoring. Reassess vitals within 1 hour. Consider blood cultures.
LOW (< 0.40):                Continue routine monitoring. Reassess if clinical condition changes.
